## As Any Trip of Mine Starts...

Of course, as any trip of mine starts, I woke up **way too late.**

The plan was simple. Catch the train at **5:50am**, which meant leaving home by car at around 5:30am for a 20-minute drive.

And at exactly 5:30am? I woke up. Slept through every single alarm.

Luckily, I had packed my bag the day before, so all I had to do was grab it, take some breakfast with me, and rush out the door.

And somehow, I made it to the train with just minutes to spare.

Not a bad start.

## Meeting Beau

Once I arrived at Amsterdam Centraal, I met up with **Beau**, and the energy was already there!

Because of all the adrenaline from rushing to catch my train, I was incredibly excited to start the trip.

What I didn't realize was just how tired I actually was.

The train ride itself was pretty straightforward. We sat back, relaxed, and at around 10:30am, we arrived at Paris Gare du Nord.

**Paris. Here we are!**

## Figuring Out Paris

First challenge? Public transport.

We had to buy metro tickets, but standing in front of the ticket machine, we didn't really know what we wanted or what to expect.

So naturally, we just bought an unlimited day pass.

Problem solved. Probably.

Our first destination was the hotel, which meant taking the metro toward the Arc de Triomphe.

And **DAMM;**

The moment we walked out of the station, came up from underground, and looked around us...

There it was.

The Arc de Triomphe, with the entire city stretching out around it.

It was amazing.

I've been lucky enough to experience a few cities like this before, so I was excited, but I also kind of knew what to expect.

But Beau?

**SHE WAS SPEECHLESS.**

Jumping around, smiling, full of energy, looking at absolutely everything. She was so incredibly happy that it honestly made the moment even better.

Seeing Paris for the first time through her excitement was something special.

And from there, we just started walking.

## Into the City!

First, we stopped at the hotel to drop off our stuff.

Then, without wasting much time, we headed straight back out to explore.

Our first destination was the **Champs-Élysées**.

As we walked, we noticed the beginning of a Christmas market being set up. Little wooden stalls were popping up everywhere, getting ready for the Christmas season.

And then we spotted this massive building.

**The Grand Palais.**

We stopped for a bit, took some nice pictures, and continued toward our next landmark.

The **Seine River**.

Right next to the Grand Palais was this enormous bridge, decorated with gold everywhere. It looked *expensive*.

That's the **Pont Alexandre III**, I later found out.

And on the other side of the bridge stood the **Hôtel des Invalides**.

Honestly, we were just enjoying looking around. Taking in the views, admiring the architecture, and realizing how beautiful Paris actually is.

## A Bookstore Side Quest

By this point, we'd walked around 4 kilometers nonstop, and our legs were starting to notice.

We needed a little break.

So we decided to take the bus toward the **Louvre**.

But while she was looking out of the window, Beau suddenly spotted something.

A really cute bookstore.

And of course, we HAD to go there.

So we got off the bus as quickly as possible and went shopping.

Beau found a really cool artistic book and some postcards. A small spontaneous stop, but exactly the kind of thing that makes exploring a city so much fun.

And somehow, we finished just in time to catch the next bus!

## The Louvre

We finally made it to the **Louvre**.

At this point, we were more than ready for that little break we'd been looking for.

We sat down, relaxed, and just enjoyed being there.

And of course, we took pictures.

Some of the best pictures of the Louvre you could ask for.

![](/blogimages/paris/louvre.jpg)

The whole place was beautiful. The buildings, the open space, and just how massive everything felt.

Definitely worth the stop.

## Our Hotel Room!

After all that walking and exploring, we took the metro back to our hotel.

And finally, our room was ready.

**AND DAMM.**

We got a really nice, big room!

It even had this cute little chill area poking out of the building, with a lovely view over the city.

Honestly, such a nice place to come back to after an entire day of walking around Paris.

We spent some time relaxing before heading out again.

![](/blogimages/paris/hotelview.jpg)

## Dinner and Good Night!

Soon enough, we went out to get some dinner nearby.

After eating, we walked around the neighborhood for a bit more, just enjoying Paris in the evening.

Nothing too crazy. Just a nice, calm way to finish the day.

And before we knew it, it was already 11pm and we were back at the hotel.

From nearly missing my train at 5:50am to spending the entire day exploring Paris with Beau, it had been a really good start.

And we still have two more days ahead of us.

**GOOD NIGHT!**

## A Good Morning

We started off strong and early!

After waking up, we headed straight downstairs for breakfast, which was included in our hotel.

Croissants, pancakes, and a nice warm cup of tea.

Honestly, a pretty great way to start the day.

Yesterday, we'd been searching around for some nice artistic districts in Paris, and we came across **Montmartre**.

So that was our first destination!

## Montmartre

One thing I really love about our hotel's location is that nothing feels far away.

Just **10 minutes on the metro**, and we arrived in a street that was absolutely MADE for tourism.

Every other building was a gift shop.

And every single one seemed to sell the exact same little Eiffel Tower keychains for 50 cents.

We started making our way toward the main landmark of Montmartre, the **Basilique du Sacré-Cœur**.

But first, we had to get there.

## The Never-Ending Stairs

On our way toward the basilica, we got stopped by some people trying to sell us bracelets.

And they were quite aggressive about it.

One guy even grabbed my arm and tried holding me there while asking where we came from.

We quickly got separated for a moment, but just kept walking and ignored them.

Not exactly the welcome we were looking for.

But soon enough, we reached the stairs.
And Damm those stairs kept coming. 

This enormous staircase leading all the way up to the basilica.

As we climbed higher and higher, we started realizing just how incredible the view was becoming.

And once we reached the top...

## Looking Over Paris

![](/blogimages/paris/artdistric.jpg)

**WOW.**

We were so high above the city, looking out over Paris with buildings stretching into the distance.

It was beautiful.

And to make the moment even better, there was a gentleman singing **Hallelujah** in the background.

Just standing there, listening to the music, looking out over the city.

It was one of those moments where everything just felt right.

Honestly, perfect.

## Getting Lost Again

After enjoying the view, I tried my best to get us away from all the tourist areas.

And somehow... We ended up in even MORE tourist areas.

We walked through the little streets of Montmartre, seeing tourist trains passing by and eventually finding this really cute square.

There were artists everywhere!

People sitting on the street drawing portraits of tourists, with little paintings and artwork displayed all around them.

It was such a nice atmosphere.

And on our quest to find the outskirt of paris, we found a cute little café.

## Chess With Beau

We went inside, ordered some tea and a hot chocolate, and decided to play some chess.

Now, according to Beau, this was one of her first times playing.

But I have my doubts.

Because she was doing Good!

Seriously, she was making moves that had me questioning whether she'd secretly been practicing. HAHA.

And just outside, the view was still unreal. One of those moments where Montmartre just kept looking better the longer we stayed there.

![](/blogimages/paris/amazingview2ndday.jpg)

We sat there for quite some time, enjoying our drinks, playing chess, and just taking a little break from walking around.

It was really nice.

## The Beau=Beau Documentary

After our little café stop, we continued doing what we've apparently become really good at. Wandering around without much of a plan.

Of course, we were also looking for some nice aesthetic places to film and take pictures for the eventual documentary of *"Beau=Beau"*.

And while searching, we stumbled upon some really cozy vinyl stores.

I love places like these.

Just shelves full of records, old music, and random things you wouldn't expect to find.

I even came across a *GTA 6 vinyl*. A day after GTA released. INSANE.

## Street Valley
Something we started noticing about Paris is how entire streets seem to be dedicated to one specific type of store.

And somehow, we ended up in a street filled with fabric shops.

One after another.

Fabric everywhere.

There were even massive groups of people crowded around one particular store because of a huge sale.

And of course, we were interested.

We went inside a few stores, looked around, and eventually bought quite a bit.

We also came across what looked like an artistic bookstore or library of some sort.

And naturally, we had to go inside.

By the time we were done, we were heading back to the hotel with: 4m² of fabric, A new hat for me, Some really nice art pieces. And a bunch of little gifts and souvenirs

That's what makes these little side quests so much fun. Not knowing what were going to discover.

## A Quick Recharge
Back at the hotel, we finally had some time to relax.

A quick shower, charging our phones and other devices, and a little rest.

But we weren't done for the day.

Not even close.

Because there was still one very important landmark we hadn't properly visited.

**The Eiffel Tower.**

And this time, we wanted to see it at night.

## The Eiffel Tower

Again, we didn't really have a plan.

We just knew we wanted to see the Eiffel Tower up close, all lit up in the dark.

So we headed out.

And when we finally got there...

**IT WAS BEAUTIFUL.**

The scale of the entire thing was already impressive, but seeing it covered in lights, sparkling against the night sky, was something else.

We spent quite some time just walking around, looking at it from different angles and enjoying the atmosphere.

We even came across an artist drawing people in this intentionally ugly but really unique style. And of course we tried it and got a good laugh. It was hilarious.

![](/blogimages/paris/uglyart.jpg)

At some point, we also called back home, showed them where we were, and just enjoyed being there.

No rush.

No real destination.

Just vibing in Paris.

## Something Strange
While walking around the Eiffel Tower, we noticed something really strange.

There were loads of people selling little souvenirs on carpets along the streets.

Nothing unusual there.

But suddenly, almost all of them started packing up.

At the exact same time.

They grabbed their carpets, picked up their stuff, and started walking away in groups.

All looking strangely coordinated.
As if someone had given them a signal.

We had absolutely no idea what was going on.

But it was quite interesting to watch them all get away quickly.

And another thing we noticed? RATS. Lots of them.

Running across the streets, disappearing into bushes, and coming back out again.

Paris really does have everything.

## Dinner With a View
In the distance, we spotted something that looked like a small Christmas market.

Little stalls, warm lights, and people standing around eating.

So naturally, we went to check it out.

We waited in line, ordered some food, and found ourselves a nice spot near a campfire.

And there we were.

Sitting in the warmth of the fire, eating our food, with the Eiffel Tower lighting up right in front of us.

And if I say so myself...

*It didn't feel real.*

After spending the entire day  with Beau wandering through Paris, shopping in little stores, playing chess, and exploring Montmartre, we somehow ended up here.

Just the two of us, enjoying a warm meal with one of the most iconic views in the world.

It was such a nice moment.

## Calling It a Day

At around **11pm**, we finally decided it was time to head back.

We'd been out exploring almost the entire day.

And what a day it had been.

From the incredible views at Sacré-Cœur to the little artistic streets, our shopping adventures, and finally the Eiffel Tower at night.

It was a **REALLY enjoyable day.**

And somehow, we've already reached the end of Day 2.

Time is going way too fast.

Tomorrow is our final day in Paris.

## A Quiet Morning

Today started a little differently.

I woke up early and sat down in the little bay window of our hotel room, looking out over Paris.

Beau was still sleeping, and I was just sitting there, vibing, watching the city slowly wake up.

People walking to work, cars passing by, everyone starting their day while I was enjoying one of our last mornings in Paris.

It was such a nice, peaceful moment. And honestly, I could've sat there for hours.

## One Last Adventure

After another delightful breakfast at the hotel, we set off to check off the final things on our list.

We still wanted to see *Notre-Dame*, *Place des Vosges*, and *Le Marais*, along with gathering some last gifts for everyone back home.

So, once again, we headed into the metro.

And when we came back above ground, we found ourselves right in the middle of a flower market!

Flowers everywhere, and the entire place smelled amazing.

Not a bad way to start exploring.

## Notre-Dame

After walking around for a little bit, we reached *Notre-Dame*.

The cathedral itself was impressive, but what surprised me even more was the amount of people.

There were enormous lines of tourists waiting to get inside.

We decided to just walk around and admire the building from outside.

And honestly, it was already an impressive sight.

## A Smaller Church

Just after Notre-Dame, we walked around a bit more and found a smaller church.

We went inside, and it turned out to be one of the nicest little moments of the day.

There was a woman singing beautifully.

A really pure, high tone, filling the whole church.

She was singing in French, and ended it with "Ainsi soit-il." It sounded amazing.

![](/blogimages/paris/insmallerchurch.jpg)

The whole place felt calm and quiet, especially compared to the crowds outside.

Definitely one of those unexpected little moments that stay with you.

## A Little Bakery
After wandering through the streets for a while, we stumbled upon this really cute bakery.

It had such a nice view that we just HAD to stop there for lunch.

We sat down, got something to eat, and just enjoyed the moment.

![](/blogimages/paris/lunchlastday.jpg)

And that's when something hit me.

How much we've actually managed to fit into these three days.

From exploring the Champs-Élysées and the Louvre, to getting lost in Montmartre, shopping in little artistic stores, and seeing the Eiffel Tower glittering at night.

All of that in just three days.

And somehow, we still weren't done.

It was such a chill moment, just sitting there with Beau, realizing how much we'd already experienced together.

## Place des Vosges
The rest of the day was pretty simple.

Walking around, scouting some nice places, and just enjoying Paris one last time.

Eventually, we made our way to *Place des Vosges*.

It's this beautiful, enormous square with a park in the middle, surrounded by really aesthetic buildings.

There was a market too, and the whole place had such a nice atmosphere.

It was one of those places where you just want to walk around and look at everything.

And honestly, that pretty much describes our entire trip.

Just walking, discovering things, and enjoying wherever we ended up.

## The Race Back

But then... That was it. Time to go home.

We had to head back to the hotel, grab our bags, and make our way to the train station.

We got on a bus that was absolutely CRAMMED with people.

And to make things even better, we got stuck in traffic. A LOT of traffic.

At some point, we seriously started wondering if we were even going to make our train.

I tried to stay cool, put on some music, and just hoped we'd get there in time.

And somehow, we made it!

## The now
And here I am.

Sitting on the train back home, writing the final day of this blog.

Sharing a bag of chips with Beau, listening to music, and looking out the window as the scenery passes by.

It's kind of crazy how quickly these three days went.

We came here with the idea of fitting as much of Paris as possible into this little adventure.

And I think we did a pretty good job.

But what made this trip so nice wasn't just seeing all the famous landmarks. It was all those little moments in between.

Getting lost, finding random stores, playing chess in a café, sitting by a campfire looking at the Eiffel Tower, and just having an amazing time together.

*I'm really glad we did this.*

## A Special Thank You
Before ending this blog, I want to give a really special thank you to Ms. Vink, Beau's grandmother.

She sponsored this amazing trip for us, and without her, we wouldn't have been able to experience all of this together.

So genuinely, **thank you so much.**

Getting to explore a city like Paris with such a dear friend of mine is something I'll always be grateful for.

These three days have been filled with so many nice memories, little adventures, and moments I'll look back on with a smile.

This trip will always have a nice little place in my heart. 🫶

**Thank you, Paris.**
Until next time!
